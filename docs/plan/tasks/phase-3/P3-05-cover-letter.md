| Phase | Effort | Depends on | Status |
|---|---|---|---|

## Why
A 2026 study found AI-assisted letters were rated higher but did not win more interviews, because AI improved the boilerplate, not the motivation recruiters care about. So the motivation line is yours, and the rest is short and evidence-based.

## What to do
- [ ] Structure, 250–350 words: a company-specific hook you write or confirm (`motivation` field); two evidence paragraphs with claim IDs; a close covering start date or relocation.
- [ ] Phrase lint that bans stock filler ("passionate", "leverage", "delve", "I am writing to express", etc.).
- [ ] Same truth checks as P3-02 on evidence sentences.
- [ ] Output `cover-letter.md` and `cover-letter.pdf`.

## Done when
- Letters for 5 jobs are within length, pass lint and truth checks, and leave the motivation as a marked blank until you fill or confirm it.
